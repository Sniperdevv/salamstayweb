Step 5 of 12 · Cultural attributes

Declare your cultural attributes

Two rows carry an info popover explaining exactly what the toggle changes for guests.

  • Women-only floor
    A separate floor or section with its own entrance.
    What guests see

    A "Women-only" badge on your listing and a match in the women-only filter. It's stated as a fact about the space — never a judgment on other listings (§10.7).

  • Pardah-respectful photography
    A commitment to modest, respectful photos throughout your listing.
    What this means for your photos

    No people in shot — family or staff stay out of frame; windows are angled away from neighbouring homes; bathrooms and prayer areas are shown empty and tidy (guidance carries into the Photos step).
