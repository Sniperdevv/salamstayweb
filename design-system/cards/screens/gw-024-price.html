What each line covers

Four lines, and that is the whole bill. Transparent fees and tax — every rupee shown before you book or earn

  • Nightly rate Set by the host, multiplied by your nights. SalamStay adds nothing to it, and the host cannot change it once your dates are held.
  • Service fee SalamStay acts as your booking agent and charges a fixed, disclosed commission for arranging and safeguarding your stay. It's a flat agency fee shown up front — not interest, and not a hidden markup on the host's price. How our fees and taxes work
  • Payment processing (MDR) The merchant-discount rate your card or wallet network charges to process the payment. We pass it through at cost and it changes with the method you pick — the line in your breakdown is quoted for JazzCash, your saved method.
  • Sales tax · ICT (Islamabad) Charged in Pakistani Rupees. Tax lines follow the property's province — here, Islamabad Capital Territory (ICT). We collect it and remit it; none of it is ours.

Withholding tax is not on your bill. It applies to what the host is paid, is deducted from the host's payout, and never appears on a guest total.
