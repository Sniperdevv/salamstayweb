Rules, states & rationale
  • Transactional vs marketing, clearly separated — exactly as GA-076. WhatsApp/SMS sit under "Needed to host" with a plain, host-specific explanation (booking requests, guest messages, payout alerts); Marketing is its own section with a visible OFF badge — never blended into one list (PECA §COMPLIANCE F6-7).
  • Marketing is never pre-ticked. The marketing switch ships aria-checked="false" in every panel, including after "Save" — the confirmation states it explicitly ("marketing is off").
  • Transactional channels are explained, not hidden. Their switches render in an informative aria-disabled on-state with a note explaining why — a host sees exactly what's required to receive booking- and payout-critical messages, never a silently-forced toggle.
  • One account, one settings surface. Change-anytime routes to /account/settings/notifications — the same shared GA-069 preferences screen guests use, since HA-003 establishes that one SalamStay account carries both modes. No separate host-only notification screen exists in the registry, so none is invented here.
  • Consent recorded, plainly. A quiet success state restates the exact choice in a real list (glyph + text) — no confetti, no oversell — then routes forward to the checklist hub /host/onboarding.
  • No checkout stepper. A host-onboarding auth-frame screen (HA-003/HA-004 lineage), not a checkout step (§7.6a).
