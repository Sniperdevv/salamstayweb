Screen — Verification "why we ask" (GA-114)

One reusable data-handling interstitial — a bottom sheet — instantiated per document. Its framing is mutual formality, not interrogation (DESIGN §9-C/§9-D, §0.2): verification protects both the guest and the host, who is verified the same way. It answers, in plain language, who sees the document (no one — hosts and guests never see it) and how it's handled (encrypted, used only for verification), then links to the full policy for specifics — no invented retention promises. The same sheet is shown for two documents — Nikah Nama and CNIC — to prove it is one pattern, not a bespoke screen. Panels: Nikah Nama (light) · CNIC (dark) · Urdu / RTL.
