Screen — Personal info & email management (GA-047)

Edit the identity fields at /account/settings/personal — name, date of birth, phone, and email — with honest handling of the sensitive ones. Changing the phone or email verifies the NEW value first: the current one keeps working until the new one is confirmed, stated plainly, and phone changes re-run the established WhatsApp-first OTP (GA-005/006). A second email can be added but lands in a verify-pending state — never silently merged (the GA-006/009 precedent). Date of birth carries a privacy note: it confirms booking eligibility and verification, and is never shown on your profile. No journey stepper — an account screen (§7.6a). Panels: field list + name in edit (light) · phone change → OTP re-verify (light) · email management + save confirmation (dark) · Urdu / RTL.
