This commitment was made on HA-023Cultural attributes — here's exactly what it means for these photos.

  • No people in frame

    Family members and staff stay out of shot — never identify anyone by including them, even in the background.

  • Family-space discretion

    Personal or family areas — like a women-only floor — are shown empty and tidy, never candid.

  • Windows framed away from neighbours

    Angle shots so a window doesn't look directly into a neighbouring home.

Practical and dignified, not a checklist to pass — these are the same considerations any thoughtful host already applies.
