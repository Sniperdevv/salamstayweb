HW-006 — Host wizard: location, the map pin, and location precision

Step 2 of 9: the address form, a map with a centre-locked pin the host moves by dragging the map underneath it, and a precision control that chooses how narrowly the public location line is written. The map is an inline-SVG illustration on the corpus map palette — no tiles are fetched, no provider is named, no attribution strip is drawn, and no accuracy or radius figure is stated anywhere. The guest-facing circle is drawn visibly off-centre from the pin, because that is what it is. Cantonment detection is kept and grounded in COMPLIANCE_MAP.md CB1–CB6. Every /host/* route is noindex, follow. Read the header comment, then HOST-SHELL.md. Panel A is the authoritative semantic page; B–I are variants.

A · Light — wizard, step 2 of 9 · Location · address filled, pin placed, cantonment detected
The authoritative page: one h1, real landmarks, a real <form>, <main class="co-main wzbody">. The pin does not move; the map does. It stands on a flat ground mark at the exact centre and lifts a few pixels while the map is being dragged — the one thing on this surface that should feel picked up rather than pressed. The dashed circle is what guests see before they book, and it is deliberately not centred on the pin (DESIGN §8.6 draws it on a jittered centre; the caption says so in words). Pin and circle are ink, not brand — HOST-SHELL §7 caps this surface at three green roles and a map overlay is not one of them. The map box takes a border and no shadow; the two chips sitting on it take elevation.onMedia, which is exactly what that shadow exists for.

G · Dark — every role re-resolves, and the pin inverts with the theme
Dark is a theme rendering, not a second design: no second h1, no landmarks. The map palette has its own dark values (--map-*, HA-020's, carried over). Because the pin resolves through interactive.selectedFill it inverts for free — near-black on a pale map, near-white on a dark one — which a brand-green pin could not do without being brightened into a fourth green role. The chips keep an opaque bg.canvas fill and the heavier dark elevation.onMedia, which is the whole reason that token sits off the ladder.

Direction demo. The chips use inset-inline-start / inset-inline-end, so the area chip and the drag hint swap corners with the reading direction and no rule is written twice. The pin and its circle do not mirror — they are geography, not layout, and the offset is a real direction on the ground. Every digit run is .num-isolated and stays Western (Pass #31).

I · Narrow — below 820px the action bar wraps and the segments stack
The caption takes the full first line and aligns to the reading-start; Back and the primary keep the row below and push to the two edges. The segmented control stacks rather than shrinking — two-line segment labels are banned by HOST-SHELL §5, and at this width "Neighbourhood" would wrap, so the track turns vertical and every label stays on one line. The map keeps its full width and loses a little height; the pin is still centre-locked, so the drag target does not move. The bars never change: nine segments at any width. The .nar scope mirrors the real @media (max-width:820px) rules exactly.
