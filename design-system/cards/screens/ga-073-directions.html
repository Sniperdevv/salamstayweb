Screen — Directions (GA-073)

How to actually get there — a static, illustrative map drawn in CSS/SVG (muted Slate-ramp palette, no map-tile hotlinking) with a route from where you are to the door, then landmark directions the way Pakistanis give them: "after Margalla Road, turn left at the F-7 Markaz signal." Because the last stretch is often on no signal, directions save offline — the affordance and its saved confirmation are both here. A call-host fallback covers the "can't find it" moment. Panels: route + save-offline (light) · saved-offline state (dark) · اردو / RTL.

The map is hand-drawn in inline SVG on the muted map palette (generated from the Slate ramp, §8.6) — a route halo + line, a start dot and a brand pin, roads and a park blob, all on-token: no tile hotlinking, CSP-safe. Directions are landmark-first ("left at the F-7 Markaz signal", "beside the pharmacy") — how people here actually navigate — as an <ol> with turn glyphs. The last row is the arrive state. Save offline is the unsaved affordance here; the fallback is the no-dead-end call-host exit.
