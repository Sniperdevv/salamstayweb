Screen — Dispute case, guest and host (GA-107)

Respectful-mediation framing (ARCH §14.4) for when a guest and host see a stay differently. The entry screen is explicit that this is not adversarial — "when you and your host see something differently and need help resolving it," never "file against your host." Case summary collects a booking-scoped issue Select, a statement Textarea, and evidence rows. The mediation track — case opened → both sides share their view → SalamStay mediates → resolution — reuses the Verification-status semantic map (muted, non-judgmental colour, §8.3), paired with an honest both-sides-heard panel: the host shares their side too, symmetric respect. Three status states are shown in context — awaiting your evidence, under mediation, resolved (outcome stated plainly) — and the escrow tie-in reuses GA-059's exact amanah language: "the amanah hold stays in place until the case resolves." No journey stepper (§7.6a). Panels: open a case + case summary (light) · under mediation + resolved (dark) · اردو / RTL.
