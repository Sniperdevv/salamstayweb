Behaviour, cultural law & S1 gate compliance
  • Canonical property types. apartment · house · guest house · farmhouse · cottage · room — the entity-register forms, verbatim, in both English and Urdu (اپارٹمنٹ · مکان · گیسٹ ہاؤس · فارم ہاؤس · کاٹیج · کمرہ).
  • Cultural = property weight (§10.1). "Women-hosted" and "Family-only" tiles are byte-for-byte the same component as "Apartment" — same surface, radius, icon box, type. No brand-green fill, no ornament, no "religious section," no ranking. Dignity by normalcy.
  • No-alcohol as a quiet fact. The default is stated once, as a plain product note on a neutral surface — never a labelled banner and never a filter chip (there is nothing to filter, since it is universal).
  • Routes to results, no dead ends. Every category opens a preview whose single primary action is "See all" → the filtered results list (GA-025). Tiles are real <button>s; the See-all is a real <a href> to /s?type=… / /s?women-hosted.
  • Copy governance. No invented counts, ratings, or superlatives on any tile or preview — pre-launch homes carry the "New" chip only (NEVER #3).
  • On-palette only. Every color is a var() token; preview thumbs are on-palette gradient placeholders; icons are literal line glyphs (a building, a bed, a family) — never decorative.
