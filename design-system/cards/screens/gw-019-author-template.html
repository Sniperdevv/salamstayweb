SalamStay Editorial

The byline on SalamStay's help articles and the local facts on our city and area pages.

SalamStay Editorial is the publishing entity accountable for what SalamStay itself writes — not a pen name for an individual and not a department nobody can reach. Everything it publishes is bound by one sourcing policy, corrected in public when it's wrong, and dated with the day its facts were last checked. This page says what it covers, what it doesn't, and how to hold it to that.

Profile last reviewed 24 July 2026 Writes in Urdu and English

Scope

What this byline covers

Four kinds of page carry it. Each one is factual, each one is sourced under the editorial and fact-check policy, and each one can be corrected by anyone who spots an error.

  • Help articlesHow a document is verified, how a rule works, what to do when something fails. Written from the product itself.
  • The local facts on city and area pagesNamed areas, markets, masjids, distances and practical notes — the parts of those pages that aren't a host's or a system's own words.
  • The trust pagesTrust & safety, our Shariah-respectful approach, and about — narrative pages that explain how SalamStay works.
  • City guides, when they existNot yet published. When a guide is written by a person with first-hand knowledge of the city, it will carry their name, not this one.

What it does not cover

  • Legal contractsThe terms, privacy policy, refund policy and host terms are agreements, drafted as such. They are not editorial and do not carry this byline.
  • What guests and hosts writeListing descriptions and reviews are their authors' own words, shown as theirs. We normalise a title and enforce the community standards; we don't write them.

Accountability

How this byline is held to account

A byline is only worth something if you can check it and challenge it. These four things are what make this one checkable.

  • One published sourcing policy Every page under this byline follows the same rules about where a fact may come from and what happens when it can't be traced. Read the editorial and fact-check policy.
  • A public correction route and log Anyone can report an error. Corrections are marked on the corrected page and listed in a complete public log. Read the corrections policy.
  • A review date that means something Each page carries the day its facts were last checked. The date moves when we re-check the facts, and not when we fix a typo — so an old date is a real signal.
  • A reachable person, in both languages Questions and challenges go to the same place everything else does. Write in Urdu or English. A person reads every ticket.

Honesty

Why there's no photograph or personal bio here

Because there is no person to put here yet, and we would rather leave the space empty than fill it with a stock face and an invented biography. SalamStay publishes no founder names, no team bios and no photographs of people anywhere on this site — our about page says so in its own words — and this profile follows the same rule rather than quietly breaking it.

So this byline is an entity, and it is honest about being one. What it can't claim is first-hand experience of a place. An entity has not walked to the masjid at the end of the street or sat through an August evening without power. When a guide needs that kind of knowledge, it will be written by someone who actually has it, their name will be on it, and their own profile page will say who they are and how they know — checkably, not as an adjective.

Until that person exists, no personal byline appears on a SalamStay page. We are not going to invent one to look more credible.

What you can check right now Every factual page under this byline names its sources in the open — the authority behind a rule, the product screen behind a document requirement, the place behind a distance. If one of them is wrong, the correction route is public and so is the result.

A person profile publishes only when all four are true
  • The person is real, named with their consent, and reachable.
  • The first-hand basis is specific enough that a reader could check it.
  • At least one published page carries their byline.
  • They are bound by the same editorial and corrections policies as everything else.

If any one of those is missing, the profile is not published and the byline stays SalamStay Editorial. That is the whole rule: a real name or an honest entity, never a plausible-sounding person in between.
