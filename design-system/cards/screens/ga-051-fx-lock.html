It states the two figures that matter — the PKR total that is actually being booked and the ≈ USD estimate the card will be charged — then restates every assumption behind that estimate (the HA-002 "restate the assumptions" precedent) rather than printing a market rate. The countdown is a plain 15-minute lock; expiry is a calm re-quote, never a penalty — nothing is lost, the rupee price never moves.
