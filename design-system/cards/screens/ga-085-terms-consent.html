Rules, states & rationale
  • Summary-first, never a wall of text. Three plain-language rows precede the full-document links — a guest can understand the shape of the agreement without reading two legal documents first.
  • Reused, not reinvented. "Held in amanah" carries GA-059's escrow language verbatim in meaning; the data row carries GA-080's export/delete/NADRA-redaction concepts; the Shariah-respectful line stays at concept level and links out to /shariah-policy rather than asserting a new claim (SEO-RULES §5 — no invented Shariah-certification language).
  • Consent is explicit. The checkbox ships unchecked; Agree & continue is disabled until checked — matching GA-075's pattern exactly, for a consistent onboarding grammar.
  • Decline is dignified. A factual reason, a neutral icon, a real Browse stays exit — the same non-shaming register as GA-075's hard-stop.
  • Real routes only. /legal/terms, /legal/privacy, /shariah-policy, /home — all canonical per SEO-RULES §3.8/§3.9/§3.12. No href="#".
  • No checkout stepper. An onboarding-lineage sheet (GA-003 family), not a checkout step (§7.6a).
