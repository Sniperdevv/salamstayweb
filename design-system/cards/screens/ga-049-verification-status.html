Screen — Verification status dashboard (GA-049)

The central, non-judgmental hub where every document, its status, and its expiry live in one place — the Verification-status checklist (DESIGN §8.3). Tone is progress, not interrogation: a quiet tick for verified, a calm indeterminate feel for "In review," and a muted amber re-prompt for anything expiring — never a red X of shame (§10.6). Expiry is re-checked ahead of time so bookings never pause (ARCH §7.3). Documents shown are whatever the party type requires — a Couple example is primary; a family example carries FRC + B-Form. Incomplete verifications link straight to the GA-115 resume flow. Panels: Couple hub · expiry re-prompt · Family hub with a needs-attention row (dark) · Urdu / RTL.
