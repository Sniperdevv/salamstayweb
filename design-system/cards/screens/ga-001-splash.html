Rules, states & rationale
  • No spinner — ever. A spinner on launch reads as "slow / broken." The splash is static and weightless (no video, no loop) and hands off to the home skeleton, which streams in.
  • Skeleton hand-off. The skeleton mirrors GA-016 card geometry exactly (zero CLS); its shimmer is transform/opacity-only and stops under reduced-motion.
  • ≤1.5s budget. Cold-start visible content targets ≤1.5s on the Tecno Spark 10 bar. Splash renders from the bundle with the system-font fallback, so first paint needs no web-font download.
  • Force-update gate. Below the minimum supported build, launch routes here before first paint — a calm blocking modal, one Update now action, never a broken app.
  • Maintenance. Reassuring, account-safe copy + a single Try again. No alarm, no error dump (§10.8).
  • Reduced-motion. Wordmark fade collapses to a single cross-fade at instant (120ms); no motion is required to proceed. Springs disabled.
  • No dead ends. Every blocking state (force-update, maintenance) names its one next action.
  • A11y / RTL. Wordmark carries aria-label="SalamStay"; blocking screens are focus-managed role="dialog"; RTL mirrors layout but never the wordmark.
