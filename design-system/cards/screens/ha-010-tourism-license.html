Screen — Tourism license capture (HA-010)

The host records their provincial tourism-license number, issuing authority and expiry — required to host legally in their area (host-verify, ARCH §11.5.4). The issuing authority is a Select that is factually city/province-matched (never an invented body name); license number and expiry date are plain fields; the license itself uploads to a neutral CSS placeholder with two equal paths in (upload a file / take a photo — capture is a convenience, never a gate, §8.8/§9-D). A WHY panel states the rule and its one consequence plainly: the listing auto-pauses if the license lapses. Case used throughout: host Aqib, listing Gulberg 2 Residence, Lahore, Punjab — continuing the lineage already shipped in HA-004 and HA-046.
