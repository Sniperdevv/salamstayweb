Behaviour, cultural law & S1 gate compliance
  • One title, hierarchical. A single document h1 (this card); each state's app-bar "Explore" is an h2, every rail an h3. Listing titles are text inside the card anchor, not headings — matching the real listing-card component.
  • Real anchors everywhere. Search pill, city tiles, listing cards, "See all", notifications and every tab bar destination are <a href> to plausible app routes (/s/stays-in-{city}, /rooms/{id}). No clickable divs; the heart and dismiss are real <button>s.
  • Cultural = Wifi weight. Halal kitchen, Prayer space, Qibla, Women-hosted, Near masjid all render as neutral bg.raised pills — never brand-green filled, never ornamented. Collections are ordinary categories beside "Popular in Islamabad". No-alcohol default stays implicit — never a banner.
  • Copy governance. Only SEO-RULES §5 registry claims or plain descriptive text; no invented ratings, review counts, or social proof — listings show the Rating "New" chip pre-launch, never "0.0".
  • All content in the DOM. Rails overflow-scroll but every card is present; nothing hides behind a tap. Skeleton rail is aria-busy with a visually-hidden status.
  • On-palette only. Every color resolves through a var() token; city/listing media are on-palette gradient placeholders (brand + slate + info stops) annotated with production alt patterns.
