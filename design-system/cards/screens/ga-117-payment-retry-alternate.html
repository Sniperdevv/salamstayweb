Screen — Payment retry / alternate rail (GA-117)

The "payment failed" recovery screen — never a dead end. A role="alert" banner names the specific, recoverable reason (per GA-057's declined-inline convention — never a generic "something went wrong"), then offers retry on the same rail as the primary action and an alternate-rail list reusing the checkout rail-row pattern (card · JazzCash · EasyPaisa · Raast). When a price or FX figure changed while the guest was away, a transparent re-quote shows the old and new totals side by side and asks the guest to confirm — a price never changes silently. And because no guest should be stranded, cash on arrival (GA-058) appears whenever the host allows it. The booking's dates and price stay held throughout. Dark on the card-declined variant; Urdu / RTL on the failed state.
