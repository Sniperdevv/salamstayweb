HW-007 — Publish, go live, and the traveller ↔ host switch

Four surfaces and one seam. The publish preview and the published screen render after 9 of 9 with no progress bars and no caption — the post-flow rule, exactly as the guest post-book screens carry no stepper. /host/today shows the state that actually exists for a first-time host: one listing waiting to be published and no reservations yet. And panel B draws the thing the founder asked for — switching from traveller to host on your profile once you are logged in — as one account in two modes, with the account menu open on the guest header and the way back always visible on the host one. The switch never re-runs verification. Panel A is the authoritative semantic page; B–H are variants.
