What these deductions mean
Service fee (wakala)
An agency (wakala) commission SalamStay charges for listing your stay, handling the booking, and holding payment in escrow until check-in. It is a fixed 3% of the gross booking value, shown before every payout.
Payment processing (MDR)
The merchant discount rate — the card-network and acquirer (HBL) fee to process the guest's card payment. It is passed through at cost, not marked up.
Withholding tax · filer vs. non-filer
Advance tax collected under FBR rules and deposited against your account. You're on the Active Taxpayers List (ATL), so the filer rate applies. Non-filers are withheld at roughly double, and a withholding certificate is issued for every payout. Update your ATL status.
