Rules, states & rationale
  • Consent is explicit. The checkbox ships unchecked; Agree & continue is a disabled affordance until it's checked — never a pre-ticked box, never an implied consent from merely opening the sheet (§10.9 optionality is explicit).
  • Reused, not reinvented. The claim CNIC-verified guests and hosts via NADRA Verisys and the "mutual formality" framing are carried verbatim in meaning from GA-041's shipped intro — same voice across the verification family (SEO-RULES §5).
  • What's shared, plainly. A popover names the exact fields (name, CNIC number, date of birth) sent for a one-time match — never the full record, never shown to hosts or other guests.
  • Decline is dignified, never a dead end. The hard-stop names the honest reason ("hosts can trust who they're welcoming"), never a verdict on the person; a neutral icon, not an alarm; Browse stays is a real, working exit; the guest can return and consent anytime from Verification (COMPLIANCE F8).
  • No checkout stepper. This is an onboarding/verification-entry sheet (GA-003 lineage), not a checkout step — it carries no numbered stepper (§7.6a).
  • Real routes only. /home (browse) and /verify (Verification hub) — no href="#", no clickable divs.
