A read-only surface showing the host's FBR Active Taxpayer List (ATL) status and its effect on the withholding rate applied to their payouts (host-verify, ARCH §6.9). Both Filer and Non-filer are shown calmly, at the same visual weight — non-filer is a status, not a verdict, and carries no warning/error color (DESIGN §10.6). Every state carries a re-check affordance ("checked against the FBR Active Taxpayer List"), an info popover explaining the ATL in one plain paragraph, and — only where relevant — a factual, no-tax-advice "become a filer" guidance row.
