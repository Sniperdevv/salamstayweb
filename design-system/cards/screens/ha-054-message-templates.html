Screen — Message templates & scheduled messages (HA-054)

Aqib's saved replies at /host/messages/templates, reachable from the HA-053 composer. Every template is bilingual by construction — an English and an اردو variant, both visible on the row, so a host is never guessing what a guest actually receives. Variable chips ({guest name}, {check-in time}, {listing name}) are inserted into a plain Textarea and fill in at send time. Scheduling is a calm utility: day before arrival, 10:00 AM, with the prayer-window-aware note carried over from GA-069's quiet-hours precedent — stated as a scheduling fact on a neutral surface, with a functional clock glyph and no religious ornament (§10.1–§10.3). The queue shows what will go out and lets a host cancel with an Undo toast (GA-090), never a confirm modal. Panels: saved replies + editor (light) · schedule + queue with Undo (light) · dark · اردو / RTL.

The prayer-window rule is written as plain scheduling behaviour, not as a devotional feature: neutral bg.raised surface, the same functional clock glyph the time field uses, and one factual sentence — a message due during a prayer window delivers just after (GA-069's quiet-hours precedent, §10.3: no brand-green cultural fill, no ornament). Cancelling a queued send is immediate and reversible — the row leaves and an Undo toast appears (GA-090) rather than an "Are you sure?" dialog.
