HW-003 — Host wizard: multi-select and switch rows

Two steps on one card, because they are the two halves of one distinction. Step 4 (/host/listings/new/amenities) is a host declaring facts — the option card with a square mark, in the 2-up grid. Step 8 (/host/listings/new/house-rules) is a host setting policy — the switch row, new to web and needed by four steps, drawn here as the segmented control at switch scale with an ink on-state and a sentence that rewrites itself with the state. No alcohol sits between No smoking and No parties; Families only and Women guests only sit in Who can book, both off, described purely as mechanics. Panel A is the authoritative semantic page; B–H are variants.

A · Light — step 8 of 9 · House rules · filled, primary enabled
The authoritative page: one h1, real landmarks, a real <form>, <main class="co-main wzbody">. Scroll it — the action bar is really sticky. Every switch is a native <input type="checkbox" role="switch"> inside its row's <label>, so the whole row is the hit target and the browser owns the state. The on-state is ink, never green: this surface spends green on the wordmark dot, the bar state and the one enabled primary, and eight green switches would be eight more. Read the second line of every row. It is not a description of the control, it is the sentence a guest will read — which is why a host never has to work out what a toggle means, and why No alcohol needs no explanation beyond sitting between No smoking inside and No parties or events at the same weight.

B · The switch row, all three renderings side by side
The component this card introduces, drawn once at full size. Off is .seg's resting track at switch scale: bg.sunken fill, inset 0 0 0 1px border.default, knob border.strong. On is .seg's selected segment: interactive.selected fill, ring removed, knob interactive.selectedFg. That is the whole colour story, and it is the same story the option card and the segmented control already tell — ink, never brand. HA-022 and HA-030 both fill the on-state green; that is not carried forward, because HOST-SHELL §7 gives this surface three green roles and a page of switches would be eight more. Focus rings the whole row, because the whole row is the hit target. Three signals distinguish on from off and only one of them is colour: the knob's position, the track's weight, and the row's second line, which is rewritten by state. .is-focus is card-only and never reaches product markup.

C · Light — step 4 of 9 · Amenities · sectioned multi-select, six ticked
The other half of the distinction. A host here is declaring a fact about the property, so the control is the option card, not a switch — same anatomy as step 1, with the mark's radius dropped from full to sm. Circle = one of these. Square = any of these. That is the only difference, and it means multi-select needs no second component. Grouping is the shell's own .fsec rhythm — three sections with a rule and a 22/600 heading — not HA-022's uppercase micro-label, which is an eyebrow and TASTE §7 has none of them anywhere. Descriptions appear only where the label is genuinely ambiguous; "TV — guests can watch television" is padding, and padding on twelve rows is a screenful of nothing. Note what is not collected here: backup power and parking, which step 5 collects properly, and which would let a listing contradict itself if both steps asked.

D · Light — step 4 · nothing ticked · primary disabled but visible and in place
The disabled primary keeps its size, its position and its label; only its fill and label colour change (bg.raised + text.disabled, TASTE §11.7). "Tick at least one" would be a dead end for a plain room that genuinely has none of the twelve, so the step ships an explicit escape hatch and the caption names both ways out. Same honesty affordance HW-001 gives load-shedding — the host is never forced to claim something that is not true, and the consequence of the honest answer is stated in plain words before they choose it. E · Light — step 8 · Continue pressed with quiet hours switched on and no end time
Both registers, as HOST-SHELL §11.3 requires: a page-level .banner.error above the form with a named recovery action, and an inline .errline under the field at fault, with .sel.err on that one control. This is the error register because it is a fact about the form; a document review outcome takes the warning register instead, and there is never a red mark on a person or a family document. The primary stays enabled — the host pressed it to get here, so it must stay pressable. Note the second recovery the error names: turning the switch off is as valid an answer as filling the field, and saying so is what stops a host inventing a time to get past a form.

F · Dark — the switch inverts with the ink token, and stays legible either way
Dark is a theme rendering, not a second design: no second h1, no landmarks. Watch the switch. interactive.selected flips from near-black to near-white and its knob flips with it, so an on switch stays the highest-contrast object in its row in both themes without ever reaching for green. The off track flips from bg.sunken-on-white to bg.sunken-on-near-black, which is nearly the canvas colour in dark — that is what the 1px border.default ring is for, and why the ring is removed only when the fill arrives to replace it. Both states clear 3:1 against their own track in both themes.

G · اردو · RTL — the switch knob travels the other way, and it needs a rule to do it
Direction demo, and the one place the switch needs an explicit rule. The track, the icon disc and the row all mirror for free because they are flex children. The knob does not. Its resting inset is logical (inset-inline-start) so it starts at the reading-start edge correctly, but its travel is translateX, which is physical — without .rtl .swrow:has(input:checked) .sw i {translateX(-18px)} the knob slides straight off the end of the track. Same class of bug as the Back chevron's scaleX(-1) in HW-001, and worth naming because the next author to build a toggle will hit it. Every digit run is .num-isolated and stays Western (Pass #31); the caption keeps HA-019's Urdu grammar, مرحلہ N از 9 · {نام}.

H · Narrow — below 820px the switch row keeps its shape and the action bar wraps
The switch row needs no narrow variant: the icon disc and the track are fixed, the body is the only flexible column, and the sentence simply wraps. That is the argument for a leading icon and a trailing control over any layout that puts the switch under the label. The quiet-hours sub-field drops its 49px indent and keeps its two columns. The caption takes the full first line and aligns to the reading-start; Back and the primary keep the row below and push to the two edges. Nine bars at any width, because the step count is a fact about the flow, not about the viewport.
