About SalamStay

SalamStay is a home-sharing marketplace for Pakistan. We list verified homes and rooms in six cities, show the facts that actually decide a stay here — who is verified, what a home allows, when the power is out — and hold your payment in amanah until you check in. This page says plainly where we are and what we claim.

Why we exist

Why SalamStay exists

Booking a place to stay in Pakistan can mean explaining yourself. A couple is asked for a marriage certificate at a front desk with a queue behind them. A family arrives to find nobody mentioned the load-shedding schedule. A woman travelling for work reads a listing three times trying to work out whether it is a place she can actually stay. None of that is exotic — it is just Tuesday.

SalamStay exists so those things are handled before you arrive, quietly, in the product. Verification happens once, in the app, matched to the booking you're actually making. House rules, cultural attributes and power backup are stated on the listing, not discovered at the door. The awkward conversation is replaced by a field.

Dignity comes from normalcy, not from a special frame.

That is the design rule the whole product is built on. A halal kitchen appears on a listing at exactly the visual weight of Wifi. Qibla direction is travel information, presented like check-in time. Women-only is a host's stated policy, not a mode we switch on for you. Nothing cultural is decorated, framed, or sold — it is simply stated, because treating it as ordinary is the respectful move.

And there is one test we hold every screen against: a single mother and her children must feel safest here. Calm, legible, honest, private, never alarmist. If a design passes that test it usually passes every other one.

The whole flow

How SalamStay works

Three steps, and no step hides anything from the next one.

1 · Search a city

Pick a city and your dates, then filter on what actually matters: halal-kitchen, prayer-space, and Qibla direction shown on listings, women-only stays, load-shedding hours and backup power.

2 · Verify once

CNIC-verified guests and hosts via NADRA Verisys. A document is asked for only when a host's rule applies — a Nikah Nama, an FRC, a B-Form — and your host never sees it.

3 · Pay, and stay

Every rupee of fees and tax is shown before you confirm. Your payment is held in amanah, a trust, and released to the host only after you've checked in.

What we say

The nine things we claim

These are the only claims SalamStay makes. They appear in the same words on every page, in the app, and in search results. If a sentence isn't on this list, we don't say it.

  1. 1 CNIC-verified guests and hosts via NADRA VerisysThe same identity check runs on both sides of every booking.
  2. 2 Nikah Nama–verified couples' bookingsConfirmed privately in the app, never shown to the host.
  3. 3 FRC-verified family bookingsFor mixed-gender siblings, using the NADRA Family Registration Certificate.
  4. 4 No-alcohol listings by defaultHosts who allow alcohol must explicitly opt in and disclose it.
  5. 5 Women-only stays hosted by womenAn option a host sets for her own home, stated on the listing.
  6. 6 Halal-kitchen, prayer-space, and Qibla direction shown on listingsPlain attributes, at the same visual weight as any other amenity.
  7. 7 Listings show load-shedding hours and backup powerBecause the hours and the generator are what decide a stay in August.
  8. 8 Two-way reviews and 24/7 Urdu + English supportGuests and hosts review each other; a person reads every ticket.
  9. 9 Transparent fees and tax — every rupee shown before you book or earnThe service fee, payment processing and tax are itemised before you commit.

This list is closed on purpose. It keeps marketing honest, it keeps the app and the website saying the same thing, and it means you can check any claim on this site against the product rather than against a mood.

Local by construction

Built in Pakistan, for Pakistan

Not a global product with a Pakistan setting. The decisions below are the ones a marketplace built anywhere else would not have made.

  • Load-shedding hours on every listingAlong with what the backup power actually runs. It is the first thing a guest asks and the last thing most listings mention.
  • Priced and paid in Pakistani RupeesCard, JazzCash, Easypaisa and Raast — the ways people here actually pay, with the currency spelled out rather than left to a symbol.
  • Urdu and English at parityNot a translation bolted on afterwards. The Urdu product is right-to-left, in Nastaliq, and carries the same facts in the same words.
  • Local compliance, handled for youTourism licensing and cantonment permissions sit inside host onboarding, and short-stay guest registration is filed with the local police on your behalf.

This page names no individuals and shows no photographs of a team, because we would rather publish what you can check. Everything above is about the product — you can open a listing and see whether it is true.
