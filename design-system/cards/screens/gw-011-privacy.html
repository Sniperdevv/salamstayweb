Section 1

Scope

This policy covers the personal information SalamStay handles when you browse, book a stay, or host one — on the website and in the apps. It applies to guests and hosts alike.

It sits alongside the Terms of Service, which govern the account itself, and the cookie policy, which covers what is stored in your browser.

Section 2

Section 3

Your NADRA verification

The identity check is a one-time match. Exactly three fields are sent for it, and nothing else:

  • NameAs it appears on your CNIC.
  • CNIC numberYour 13-digit identity number.
  • Date of birthConfirms the match against NADRA's record.
  • Nothing beyond thisNever shared with hosts or other guests.

Your CNIC is encrypted and used only for verification — hosts and guests never see it. Your CNIC number is never shown on your profile or to anyone you book with, and your NADRA documents stay redacted, always — including in the copy of your data you can download for yourself.

Section 4

How consent works here

Nothing is ticked for you. Every consent box on SalamStay starts empty, and the button that follows it stays inactive until you tick it yourself. That is true of the terms box at sign-up and of the NADRA box before verification.

If you decline the identity check, you're told plainly what that means rather than being pushed: you can still browse stays — you just won't be able to book until you consent. You can consent anytime from Verification.

Messages: what's needed, and what's optional

WhatsApp and SMS — needed for your bookings Booking confirmations, verification codes, messages from your host; SMS is the backup channel if WhatsApp can't reach you. These two stay on — they carry the messages every booking needs, and you'll always be told why before one arrives. Always on
Offers & tips — marketing Occasional deals and travel ideas, by WhatsApp or SMS. Off unless you turn it on. Turning it on is entirely your choice, and never needed to book or host. Off
Why it's built this way This meets Pakistan's Electronic Crime Act (PECA) rules for consent: transactional messages are disclosed plainly, and marketing is opt-in only. Change this anytime in Notification & channel preferences.

Section 5

What we never share

Three things are kept private, always — not as a setting you have to find, but as a rule of the product.

  • Your exact location is never shown before a booking is confirmedUntil then, a home shows as a general neighbourhood.
  • Your phone and email are never shown to other membersMessages go through SalamStay, so your contact details stay yours.
  • Your personal information is never soldNot to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.

Section 6

Your privacy defaults

You're in control of what others can see and who can reach you. We start you on the more private choice — you don't have to go hunting through settings to be safe by default.

  • Who can see your profile — hosts you book withOnly hosts of a stay you request or book can see your profile.
  • Who can message you — hosts with a bookingOnly a host you've requested or booked with can start a chat. You can still block anyone.
  • Approximate locationYour area shows as a general neighbourhood until a booking is confirmed.
  • Blocked peoplePeople you block can't message or book with you, and are never told they were blocked. Unblock any time.

Section 7

Export and delete

Getting a copy of your data

We'll gather your bookings, messages, reviews, and profile into one file you can download — as JSON, which keeps the full structure, or as CSV if you'd rather open it in a spreadsheet. Preparing it takes a little while; we'll message you when it's ready. The file stays available for a limited period and is then deleted for you — the exact date is shown beside the download.

Your NADRA documents aren't included. Your CNIC and family verification documents stay redacted — they aren't part of the export, even for you.

Deleting your account

You can close your SalamStay account whenever you like. Here's exactly what happens.

  • Your profile and saved places are removedProfile, wishlists, drafts, and messages are deleted.
  • Completed bookings are kept, anonymizedBookings you've completed are retained in anonymized form, as the law requires — not linked to you.
  • Your NADRA documents stay redactedThey were never readable to us, and they're deleted with your account.
  • Finish any active trip or payout firstDeletion waits until nothing is in flight.
You can change your mind You have 14 days to change your mind — just sign back in and your account is restored, nothing lost. After that, deletion is permanent.

Section 9

How long we keep things

Two retention facts are settled today, and they're stated above rather than hidden here: your downloaded data file is deleted for you after the window shown beside it, and a deleted account is restorable for 14 days before deletion becomes permanent. Completed bookings are kept in anonymized form, as the law requires.
