Behaviour, anti-doorway law & S1 gate compliance
  • Six live cities, rich and honest. The Day-1 beta (Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore, Peshawar, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi) each render as a full card with a locally-true practical hint drawn from city-facts.md — kept qualitative (no fixed load-shedding hours per §7 of city-facts) and price-free (prices are data-driven, never hard-coded).
  • Anti-doorway supply gate is the spine. Coming-soon cities (Murree, Hunza, Skardu, Swat, Multan, Abbottabad — Tier 2/3 from cities.md) are <button>s that open an explainer sheet, never anchors to /stays-in-{slug} — those pages do not exist until supply lands. No empty page is ever reachable (cities.md §0).
  • No dead ends. The coming-soon sheet names two next actions: "Notify me" and a route to the nearest live city. Northern-Areas demand is acknowledged without promising inventory.
  • Image discipline. Islamabad reuses the cached, attributed Pexels photo (is-f7-view.jpg, referenced relatively). The other five carry build-time image-slot comments with full intent specs (subject, mood, orientation, palette fit, cultural constraints) for a Pexels-first fetch later — gradient placeholders stand in now, no speculative 5-photo pull.
  • Cultural & on-palette. No ornament anywhere; the coming-soon chip and tiles use neutral surfaces, never brand-green (which would read as an endorsed rank). Every color is a var() token.
