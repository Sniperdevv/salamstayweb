Behaviour, the PK advantage & S1 gate compliance · web-variant note

  • The PK advantage: CNIC re-verify. Because identity was CNIC-verified at sign-up, a fully locked-out guest is never stranded — they re-run the GA-041 CNIC + selfie capture and a human matches it to the account. Timeframe is stated honestly ("a few hours", human-reviewed); the privacy line says the CNIC is used only for this recovery and never shown to hosts (§10.6).
  • Human escalation is always one tap away. "Message our team" (/help/account-recovery/contact) sits on the entry, the fallback, and the failed state — with a plain note that support answers in Urdu + English and around prayer times (host-support §1.2 tone), never a promise we can't keep.
  • Recovery hands back a stronger account. Success is a quiet tick, then an optional secure-your-account nudge: reset password (recommended), turn on two-step (GA-010), review sessions (GA-079). All skippable via "Continue".
  • No dead ends, no blame, no fear (journeys §3.2, §10.6/§10.8). The failed state explains why ("used already, or mistyped"), reassures ("nothing has changed"), and offers three live exits. Copy never accuses the guest and never fear-markets the lockout.
