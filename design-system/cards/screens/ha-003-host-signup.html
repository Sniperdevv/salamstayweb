Rules, states & rationale
  • Role-switch is primary, honestly framed. "Your SalamStay account works for both; your guest verification carries over" is stated plainly and proven by the identity card's real "CNIC verified" chip — never a vague promise (journey gap #6 folded in, SCREENS §1.2 Stage 1).

  • Urdu Nastaliq input, demonstrated not just claimed. Panel D's name field shows a real Nastaliq-set name ("احمد رضا") to prove the field accepts Urdu input directly, not just that the UI translates.
  • Real routes only. /account, /become-a-host, /host/onboarding, /legal/host-terms, /legal/privacy (+ /ur/ counterparts). No href="#", no clickable divs.
  • No dead ends. Both paths' primary buttons are always active affordances (no invalid-until-filled state modeled here — OTP/validation states are GA-004/GA-005's job, reused not redesigned); the confirmation state's only forward action is real and working.
