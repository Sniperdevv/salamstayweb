Popular questions
How is my payment held until check-in?

Your payment is held in amanah — a trust — with Meezan Bank, and released to the host only after you've checked in. It isn't spent, lent out, or used to earn interest while it waits.

What is the service fee (wakala)?

The service fee is a wakala — an agency commission — shown as its own line in your price breakdown before you book. It's never folded into the total or hidden.

How does CNIC verification work?

We check your CNIC through NADRA Verisys. If something needs another look, we'll tell you exactly what to fix — never just "failed."
