The Raast rail — Pakistan's State Bank instant-payment system — as a bank-to-bank transfer inside checkout. The guest picks their bank (real PK banks as plain text rows, no invented logos), then pays either through their Raast ID (an alias linked to their account) or a full IBAN, confirms an instant transfer, and settles in seconds.
