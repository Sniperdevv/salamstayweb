How your money is held

When you pay, your money doesn't go straight to the host. We hold it in amanah — a trust — and release it only after you've checked in. It protects both sides: you know the host is paid once you've arrived, and the host knows the money is really there.

Step by step
You payYour payment leaves your card or wallet in Pakistani Rupees.
Held in amanahIt sits in a custody account at Meezan Bank — not spent, not lent out, no interest earned on it.
You check inOnce your stay begins and check-in is confirmed, the hold is released.
The host is paidThe stay amount is sent to the host. You get your receipt in the app.
Why Meezan Bank
Custody without interest (riba-free)

Meezan is an Islamic bank, so your money is held in a custody arrangement, not a lending one — it isn't put to work to earn interest while it waits. It's simply kept until it's due.

If you cancel

Because the money is still held, your refund comes straight back from that hold — no waiting on the host — for whatever the stay's cancellation policy allows.

Common questions
Does SalamStay use my money while it's held?

No. It sits in the custody account untouched. We don't spend it, lend it, or earn interest on it — it's yours until it's released to the host or refunded to you.

When exactly is the host paid?

After you've checked in. The hold releases once your check-in is confirmed, so the host is paid for a stay that has actually started — not before.

Is a receipt issued for the held amount?

Yes. You get a confirmation the moment you pay, and a full receipt once the stay amount is released to the host. Both live in Your trips.
