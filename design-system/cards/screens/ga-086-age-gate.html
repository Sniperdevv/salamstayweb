Rules, states & rationale
  • Gregorian, not Hijri. This is a legal eligibility check, not a cultural date surface — day/month/year boxes read a Gregorian date only, matching a CNIC/B-Form.
  • Factual why-line. "SalamStay accounts are for adults 18 and over" states the rule once, plainly, before the field — never buried in fine print after the fact.
  • Privacy note reused, not reinvented. Carries GA-047's shipped concept verbatim in meaning: date of birth confirms eligibility and verification, and is never shown on the guest's profile.
  • Under-age is warm, never shaming. A neutral icon, a factual rule restatement, a concrete return year, and no characterisation of the person — the tone matches GA-113's dignified hard-state framing (§10.6).
  • Correction path. "Enter a different date" stays one tap away — a genuine typo shouldn't read as a permanent lockout.
  • Browse-only exit. "Browse stays" is a real, working link (/home) — the block never dead-ends the guest.
  • No checkout stepper. An onboarding auth-frame screen (GA-004 lineage), not a checkout step (§7.6a).
