Become a host on SalamStay

SalamStay is a home-sharing marketplace for Pakistan. List a room or your whole home for CNIC-verified guests, set your own house rules and cultural preferences, and see every rupee of fees and tax before you earn. Hosting income is paid to you as rent — never interest — for the use of your home.

Getting started

How hosting works

Three steps, no signup wall to see what you could earn first — try the earnings estimator before you create an account.

1 · Create your host account

Sign up, or switch your existing SalamStay account to host mode — in English or Urdu.

2 · Verify your identity

Confirm your identity through CNIC-verified guests and hosts via NADRA Verisys, and the details of your home.

3 · List your home

Add your photos, house rules, and price — then go live for guests in your city.

Getting paid

Fees and earnings

Hosting income is rent for the use of your home — paid to you, never lent out, and never interest.

Guest payments are held in amanah — a trust — with Meezan Bank, and released to you once your guest checks in. It isn't spent, lent out, or used to earn interest while it waits.

Transparent fees and tax — every rupee shown before you book or earn. Three items come off each booking before your payout:

Service fee (wakala)SalamStay's commission for listing, booking and holding payment in escrow 3%
Payment processing (MDR)The card-network and acquirer fee, passed through at cost at cost
Withholding taxFBR advance tax — the filer or non-filer rate applies filer / non-filer

Before you list

Frequently asked questions

How does host verification work?

You confirm your identity through CNIC-verified guests and hosts via NADRA Verisys, just like every guest who books with you. It's a short, mutual formality — you're trusted by default, and if anything needs fixing we tell you exactly what and why.

Is the income I earn from hosting halal?

The money you earn from hosting is rent for the use of your home — never interest, never riba. Guest payments are held in amanah, a trust, with Meezan Bank until your guest checks in, then released to you.

What fees does SalamStay charge hosts?

A service fee (wakala) of 3% of the booking, payment processing (MDR) passed through at cost, and withholding tax at the filer or non-filer rate. Transparent fees and tax — every rupee shown before you book or earn.

Can I choose who books my home?

Yes. You set your own house rules and party-type acceptance — including women-only stays hosted by women — and no-alcohol listings by default, unless you explicitly opt in and disclose otherwise.
