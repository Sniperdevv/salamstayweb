Screen — Offline & sync queue (GA-088)

The standalone offline mode for flaky PK data (§7.6a: no journey stepper). A muted offline banner sits at the top — calm, never an error-red alarm — over a list of queued actions (an arrival confirmation, a message to Ayesha, a saved review draft), each with a plain per-item status. The auto-retry promise is stated exactly, in the GA-091/GA-115 language: "we'll send these the moment you're back online — nothing is lost." When the connection returns, items flip to Sent with a toast, and the all-synced state is a warm "You're all synced." Poor signal is a first-class state, not a failure (§9-D resilience). The offline banner uses the muted register (bg.raised + a wifi-off glyph), never semantic.error — being offline is a state, not a failure (§9-D). Queued actions carry a plain per-item status by glyph + text: Will send (a warning-tint clock, for things headed to the server) vs Saved (a neutral check, for a local draft that stays put until you finish it) — the distinction is honest, a draft is not silently "sent." The reassurance strip states the promise verbatim from GA-091/GA-115: nothing is lost, it sends on its own. Counts wrap in .num.

When the connection returns, the banner flips to the success register and the queued items restate themselves as Sent (glyph + text) on their own — no retry the guest has to remember (§9-D, mirroring GA-091). A non-blocking role="status" toast confirms delivery. The review draft stays Saved, deliberately — a draft is never auto-posted, so the honest resolution isn't "everything sent," it's "your queued sends went; your draft is still yours." The all-synced state is the warm resting state: a success check, one line, nothing to do.
