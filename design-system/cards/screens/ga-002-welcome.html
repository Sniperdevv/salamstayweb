Rules, states & rationale
  • At most 3 slides, first-run only. Each is headline + one line + one calm line glyph — no illustrations, no mascots, no ornament.
  • Claims registry only. Slide lines are SEO §5 claims verbatim (#1 CNIC/NADRA, #4 no-alcohol default, #7 load-shedding + backup power) — no invented stats, ratings, awards, or testimonials (none exist pre-launch).
  • Plain product framing. Not an "Islamic app" pitch — "stays across Pakistan, with the details that matter to you." Quiet Modern from the first frame (§9-I, §10.2).
  • Always skippable. Skip persists top-trailing at a 44pt target on every slide, including the last.
  • Ask-late, no sign-up wall. Ends on Home; Browse without an account is primary, Sign in secondary; auth only on save/book (§9-I).
  • Dots = tablist. Position is announced and the active dot elongates — never color-only (§11).
  • Motion. Slide advance is a cheap cross-fade (no horizontal slide theatrics); reduced-motion keeps it a single instant cross-fade; swipe + keyboard both navigate.
  • RTL. Order, skip/brand placement, and advance mirror; the Next chevron flips; the wordmark never mirrors.
