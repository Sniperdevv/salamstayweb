The shared variant of a saved list at /wishlists/islamabad-august — the same list GA-102 ships, now planned by three people. Wishlists are never public: collaboration is invite-only by phone number, every invite must be accepted, and there is no public link, no share-to-web control, no feed, no likes and no comments anywhere on this surface. The only collaboration chrome is a members strip of initials avatars (no faces on fictional identities, GA-090/GA-110) and one quiet attribution line per stay naming who added it. Members can save and remove freely — a removal is optimistic and reversible via the same Undo snackbar GA-102 established, and the attribution says who did it. Hearts remain real <button aria-pressed> siblings beside the card anchor, never inside it.
