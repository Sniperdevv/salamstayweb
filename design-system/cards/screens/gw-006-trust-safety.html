Trust & safety at SalamStay

SalamStay is a home-sharing marketplace for Pakistan. Every booking is verified on both sides — CNIC-verified guests and hosts via NADRA Verisys — your payment is held in amanah, a trust, until you check in, and every stay carries two-way reviews and 24/7 Urdu + English support. This page explains how each of those actually works.

Both sides, same standard

How verification works

Guests verify. Hosts verify. The same check runs on both sides of every booking, and we only ask for a document when a rule actually applies to your booking type — you are trusted by default. Documents are used to confirm the booking and are never shown to the other party.

CNIC-verified guests and hosts via NADRA Verisys

Identity is confirmed against NADRA for both sides of every booking — the same check for the person staying and the person hosting.

Nikah Nama–verified couples' bookings

Couples confirm their booking type privately with a Nikah Nama, matched to the host's house rules — not published, not shared with the host.

FRC-verified family bookings

Mixed-gender families and siblings verify with a NADRA Family Registration Certificate. Families travelling with children add a B-Form.

If a document can't be verified, we tell you exactly what to fix A check that doesn't pass is never a verdict on you. You see what was unclear and what to do next, and you can re-submit — or reach a person at contact support. Read the full detail in Verification & documents.

Payment protection

How your money is protected

When you pay, your money doesn't go straight to the host. We hold it in amanah — a trust — and release it only after you've checked in. It protects both sides: you know the host is paid once you've arrived, and the host knows the money is really there.

  1. You payYour payment leaves your card or wallet in Pakistani Rupees.
  2. Held in amanahIt sits in a custody account at Meezan Bank — not spent, not lent out, no interest earned on it.
  3. You check inOnce your stay begins and check-in is confirmed, the hold is released.
  4. The host is paidThe stay amount is sent to the host. You get your receipt in the app.

Transparent fees and tax — every rupee shown before you book or earn. The full price, fees and tax included, is visible before you commit; the same breakdown is shown to your host before they earn. Nothing is added afterwards.

Because the money is still held, a refund comes straight back from that hold — no waiting on the host — for whatever the stay's cancellation policy allows.

If you need us

Reporting, blocking and support

Two-way reviews and 24/7 Urdu + English support. Guests and hosts review each other after a stay, and help is reachable at any hour in either language. Reporting and blocking are separate tools, and you never have to justify using either one.

  • Report a listing or a personYour report goes to our safety team. Reporting is private, and it helps keep SalamStay safe for everyone — the person you report is not told who reported them.
  • Block someoneBlocking sets a clear boundary — it hides, it doesn't accuse. The blocked person is never notified. It's reversible at any time from your blocked-people list.
  • Talk to a personA person reads every ticket. Support is reachable 24/7, in Urdu or English — we don't promise a reply time we can't keep, so none is stated.
  • Community standardsWhat is and isn't acceptable on SalamStay, for guests and hosts alike — written plainly, applied to both sides of a booking.

Privacy on the trip

Trip-safety features

These are privacy defaults that help on any trip — they are on for everyone, not a separate mode you have to switch into or admit to needing. The safer option is the default on every row, and you can change any of them in your privacy settings.

  • Approximate location until a booking is confirmedA home's exact address is only shared once the booking is confirmed. Before that, the map shows the general area.
  • Share your arrival with a trusted contactAn optional switch at check-in. It shares the arrival, not a live location — and it is plainly labelled, never branded as a mode for one kind of traveller.
  • Messaging limited to the hosts you book withShips limited by default. Nobody can message you out of the blue, and your profile stays visible only to the hosts of the stays you book.
  • Women-only stays hosted by womenAn option some hosts set for their home, stated on the listing as host policy — never assumed for you, never applied to your account.

Local requirements

Guest registration with local police

Short-stay guest registration is a routine legal requirement in Pakistan — the same formality any hotel or guest house completes. SalamStay files this for you and your host; there is nothing for either of you to submit.

Islamabad Islamabad Capital Territory (ICT) Police Your stay is registered with the ICT Police for your dates. SalamStay files it with your host as soon as your booking is confirmed, and you can see the reference in your trip details.
Punjab — Lahore, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi Punjab Police — Hotel Eye Punjab stays are filed through the Hotel Eye system, the provincial short-stay registration service. Filing runs automatically within 24 hours of check-in, so a booking ahead of check-in shows as pending rather than a reference number.
What is shared, and what isn't Only the details the registration process asks for are shared — nothing more. Your host sees names and guest count with CNIC numbers masked; the full number goes to the police portal, never to another guest or host. More detail: guest registration in the help center.

When views differ

If something goes wrong

Sometimes a guest and a host see a stay differently and need help resolving it. That is what mediation is for — not a complaint filed against your host, and not a case you win. Both sides are asked for their view, and the outcome is stated plainly.

  1. Case openedYou describe what happened, scoped to one booking, and attach anything that helps.
  2. Both sides share their viewYour host is asked for their side too. Symmetric respect — neither account is treated as the default truth.
  3. SalamStay mediatesOur team reviews both statements. The amanah hold stays in place until the case resolves.
  4. ResolutionThe outcome is stated plainly — what is returned, to whom, and when — with no editorialising about who was right.
