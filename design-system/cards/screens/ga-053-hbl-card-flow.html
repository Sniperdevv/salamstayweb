Screen — HBL card flow, 3-D Secure (GA-053)

The primary card rail — debit / credit cards through the HBL acquirer, with the 3-D Secure step-up handled per the §9-C addendum. Card entry shows a masked number, a plain "why we ask" note on the CVV, and the tokenisation line (SalamStay never sees the full number). On submit the pay moment becomes a state over the confirm step, never a route: a processing overlay locks every control. When the bank issues a challenge it opens as a modal above checkout — full-height, focus-trapped, served by the bank; the frictionless path shows nothing (silent 3DS resolves under the same overlay). Panels: card entry, the 3DS challenge modal, the processing overlay (frictionless note), a calm success, and an inline recoverable failure. Dark on the overlay, Urdu / RTL on card entry.
