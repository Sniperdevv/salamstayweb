Guest refund policy

Every home carries one of three cancellation policies, set by its host and shown to you before you pay. This page is the full version of what you see there — the windows, what isn't refunded, and when the money reaches you. You always see the exact rupee amount before you confirm anything.

Section 1

Which policy applies to you

The host chooses one of three policies for their home. It's printed on the listing, shown again at booking review before you pay, and repeated in your trip details afterwards — so you never have to come to this page to find out which one you're on.

Because your payment is held in amanah until you check in, your refund comes straight back from that hold — no waiting on the host — for whatever the stay's cancellation policy allows.

Section 2

The three cancellation policies

Read across your host's row. All times are measured against your check-in, and a full refund means everything you paid comes back.

Cancellation windows by policy. "Half" means half of your nightly total returns; the service fee (wakala) is kept.
Policy Full refund Half refund No refund
Flexible Free cancellation until 5 days before check-in — everything you paid comes back. Until 48 hours before check-in. After that, or once you've checked in.
Moderate Free cancellation until 7 days before check-in — everything you paid comes back. Until 48 hours before check-in. After that, or once you've checked in.
Strict Free cancellation only within 48 hours of booking, if that's 14+ days before check-in. Until 7 days before check-in. Within 7 days of check-in, or after arrival.

A host who asks for Strict is asking for more certainty about their calendar. It isn't a judgement about you, and it's stated on the listing before you commit.

Section 3

Ramadan and Eid

A host can extend free cancellation to 10 days before check-in for stays over Ramadan and the Eid holidays, when travel plans often shift. Where a host has set this, it's shown on the listing alongside their usual policy.

Not every host offers it. A host on Strict may keep the Strict schedule during Ramadan and Eid, with no extra exception — which is also stated on the listing.

How the dates are read Boundaries follow the Hijri calendar, which is approximate and may vary by moon sighting. The exact calendar date your window closes is always shown to you in the app before you confirm.

Section 4

What isn't refunded

Two things sit outside a partial refund, and both are visible on your price breakdown before you pay — Transparent fees and tax — every rupee shown before you book or earn.

  • The service fee (wakala)Half of your nightly total returns; the service fee (wakala) is kept. SalamStay acts as your booking agent and charges a fixed, disclosed commission for arranging and safeguarding your stay — it's a flat agency fee shown up front, not interest and not a hidden markup on the host's price.
  • Nights already reserved for youNights already reserved for you are non-refundable — the home was held off the calendar for those dates.

Section 5

A worked example

A real three-night booking, cancelled after the free window closed. This is the same ledger you'd see in the app before confirming — every line, with the total.

You're cancelling Margalla View Apartment · Fri 14 – Mon 17 Aug 2026 · paid PKR 42,350
Nights 2 & 32 nights × PKR 12,500 · refunded PKR 25,000
First nightHeld per policy · non-refundable PKR 12,500
Service fee (wakala) & taxesAlready earned · non-refundable PKR 4,850
Total refundBack to the card you paid with PKR 25,000

This policy's free-cancellation window closed 7 Aug. Cancelling on 10 Aug, the first night is non-refundable — everything else comes back, exactly as above. Nothing is estimated: you see this ledger, for today's date, before you confirm.

Section 6

How your refund reaches you

Refunds return to the same card, wallet, or bank account you paid with — you don't choose a different destination, and you don't need to ask for it.

  • Card refundsMost reach you in 5–7 business days once sent.
  • Wallet refundsJazzCash and EasyPaisa refunds usually arrive sooner than card refunds — timing depends on your bank.
  • Bank transfersLocal bank transfers can take a little longer.
  • You can follow itRequested, processing, sent — each stage is dated in your trips, with the destination shown.

Cancel any time from Trips. You'll see the exact refund for today's date before you confirm — no surprises.

Section 7

If a host changes their policy

A host can change the cancellation policy on their home at any time. It never reaches back to you: policy changes apply to new bookings only — guests who already booked keep the policy they booked under.

Section 8

If you and your host disagree

Sometimes a guest and a host see a stay differently and need help resolving it. That is what mediation is for — not a complaint filed against your host, and not a case you win. Both sides are asked for their view, and the outcome is stated plainly.

  1. 1 Case openedYou describe what happened, scoped to one booking, and attach anything that helps.
  2. 2 Both sides share their viewYour host is asked for their side too. Neither account is treated as the default truth.
  3. 3 SalamStay mediatesOur team reviews both statements. The amanah hold stays in place until the case resolves.
  4. 4 ResolutionThe outcome is stated plainly — what is returned, to whom, and when.

Read more about how mediation works.

Section 9

Special circumstances

Beyond the windows above, SalamStay does not currently operate a separate extenuating-circumstances policy. If something serious happens, talk to support — a person reads every ticket — and if the stay is already in dispute, mediation is the route.

To be completed with counsel 9.1  A force-majeure and extenuating-circumstances policy Which events override the host's policy, what evidence is asked for, and who bears the cost. No such entitlement exists on SalamStay today, so none is described here — a policy that promised one would be a promise we couldn't keep at the counter.
