Screen — Payment method manager (GA-052)

The generic payment wallet at /account/settings/payment — one place to keep every Pakistan rail: a card via the HBL acquirer, JazzCash and EasyPaisa wallets, a Raast-linked bank account. The list is a default-method radiogroup (one default, changeable), each row removable, with a tokenised-storage security line stated plainly. Panels: the saved list (light + dark), the add-method sheet (rail choice with per-rail notes — card / wallet / Raast), the remove-confirm dialog, the empty state, and Urdu / RTL.
