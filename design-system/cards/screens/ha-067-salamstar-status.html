Screen — SalamStar programme status (HA-067)

The host's SalamStar surface at /host/salamstar. SalamStar is SalamStay's Superhost equivalent, and the product currently ships exactly one thing about it: the tag itself, rendered on the public host profile (GA-033) and beside listings in results (GA-025/GA-102/GA-027). No criteria, no threshold, no tier and no benefit beyond that placement exists anywhere in the corpus — so this screen does not invent one. It states the shipped placement as fact, shows the host's own checkable numbers (occupancy 78% = 54 of 69 nights, 1–23 July 2026, carried verbatim from HA-046/HA-066), and renders every unshipped criterion and benefit as a visibly empty, labelled template slot with a Not set yet chip — parked for founder sign-off. No percentile, no "hosts like you", no league table, and — per HA-062/HA-066 — no aggregate host rating anywhere. Host chrome, no stepper (§7.6a). Panels: status + own numbers (light) · criteria slots + placements (light) · status (dark) · اردو / RTL.

The whole design decision on this screen is what it refuses to say. A Superhost-equivalent surface normally leads with thresholds — "4.8+ rating, 90% response rate, top 10% of hosts". None of those exist in this product: HA-062 and HA-066 ship no host-level score at all, and no criterion, tier or percentile appears anywhere in the corpus. So the criteria render as visibly empty, monospaced, dashed-border slots with a Not set yet chip — the shape of the future row without a fabricated value in it, which is also exactly what the Next.js build will bind to. What the screen does assert is checkable: the tag's two shipped placements (profile GA-033, listing card GA-025), and Aqib's own figures reused verbatim — 78% occupancy stated as the fraction 54/69 so he can check it himself, and +6% against his own last month, never against another host. The status line is non-punitive by construction: "Not assessed yet" plus "nothing is counted against you" — a missing badge is never framed as a failure. The support row promises a person, never a response time.

Mirrored end to end — the back chevron and the row chevrons flip, the tag and the slot rows read from the right, and the percentage keeps LTR digit order inside its isolated span. Every value is a Western digit inside .num with Urdu prose around it (Pass #31 ruling), so 54/69 and 1–23 July stay readable and never digit-order-mirror. The slot tokens stay in Latin monospace on both sides — they are build-time identifiers, not prose, and translating them would break the binding.
