Behaviour, cultural law & S1 gate compliance · web-variant note
  • Photo is optional, initials are first-class. The two choices carry equal visual weight — declining a photo (a pardah-respectful choice) is never rendered as a deficiency, and the fallback is a dignified initials avatar on bg.sunken, never a generic "missing" silhouette (DESIGN §9-J / §10.7).
  • Decade-born privacy promise. DOB is captured for the 18+ age gate, but the profile only ever exposes the decade ("Born in the 1990s"); the promise is stated inline under the field, per §9-J.
  • Name framed with dignity. "As on your CNIC" is guidance that explains the benefit (smoother verification), not a demand for the card — and only the first name is ever public.
  • Skip-safe, everywhere. Every step carries "Skip for now" → /home and never blocks the guest from booking; the flow is progressive and fully reversible. Everything set here is editable later in GA-129.
  • Language at launch: EN / اردو. A real radiogroup; the اردو option is Nastaliq with lang="ur"; the ongoing switcher lives at GA-068 (DESIGN §11).
  • Real anchors, real controls. Photo capture /account/profile/photo (GA-130), editor /account/profile/edit (GA-129), settings /account/settings/accessibility, home /home. Steppers, radios and CTAs are real <button>s / anchors; no href="#", no button-in-anchor. [44pt] throughout.
  • On-palette, dignified, bilingual. Every colour is a var() token; no ornament; the initials avatar uses interactive.subtle, never a green "endorsement" fill; copy is plain descriptive with no invented stats.
  • Web variant (/onboarding/profile, SPA). The same three steps in a centred ~440px card with a top progress bar; the photo step opens a file-picker + crop modal instead of the native camera; "Skip for now" and the decade-born promise are identical.
