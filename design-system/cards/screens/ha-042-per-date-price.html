Screen — Per-date price editor (HA-042)

Where HA-041's calendar hands off a date or a range: /host/calendar/2026-08-14, in the same editor frame as ha-040/ha-043/ha-044 — app bar, listing context chip, save/discard, unsaved-changes bar, no wizard bars. The screen's whole job is to make override versus base legible: what this night would cost without an override is always on screen, the override sits beside it, and "Remove override" is a first-class action so a one-off price can never become a one-way door. The case is the one ha-043 already created — Friday 14 August 2026 at Cantt View Residence, carrying the PKR 16,500 that bulk edit applied and saved over the PKR 15,000 weekend rate. Currency follows HA-002/HA-031 exactly: ₨ is an input-field prefix adornment and nothing else — every figure in prose or in the comparison spells PKR. There is no suggested rate, no market comparison and no demand statistic: HA-031 shipped the explicit absence of smart pricing, and the weekend and Eid rows show only the host's own shipped figures, linking to the editors that own them. The ₨ glyph appears exactly once per panel — inside the input, as a unit adornment — and every other figure spells PKR: this is HA-002/HA-031's shipped rule and DESIGN §8.1's "currency is labelled, never symbol-only," applied to a screen whose entire content is money. The comparison block is what makes an override safe: A shows the override and, right beneath it, what the night would be without one; B adds the third figure a host actually needs mid-edit — about to set, currently set, and no-override-at-all — so saving is never a guess; C arrives from HA-041's selection with no override, and states plainly that two different underlying rates are in the range rather than flattening them into a single fake "current price." Remove override sits at the same weight as saving because an override is a one-off, not a commitment. There is no suggested rate anywhere: the only numbers on screen are the host's own, and both context rows link out to the editors that own them (HA-031, HA-044) instead of re-deciding them here.
