Screen — EasyPaisa wallet flow (GA-055)

The EasyPaisa mobile-wallet rail — the same app / web handoff pattern as JazzCash (GA-054). From the confirm step, the guest switches to the EasyPaisa app, approves with their PIN there, never in SalamStay (the trust boundary, stated honestly), and returns via the deep-link. Panels walk the round-trip as states over the pay moment: initiate ("you'll switch to EasyPaisa"), the app-switch interstitial (deep-link out, waiting), the returned-pending reconciliation (the GA-116 pattern — "confirming with EasyPaisa, don't close"), a calm success (no confetti), and the failure return (retry + alternate rail, the GA-117 pattern). No wallet-brand colour is used — emphasis is on-palette only. Dark on pending, Urdu / RTL on initiate.
