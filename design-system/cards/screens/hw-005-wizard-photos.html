HW-005 — Listing wizard, step 6 of 9: Photos

The photo step at web width, on hw-001's wizard chrome and under HOST-SHELL.md unchanged. It ships the dropzone, the uploading skeleton, the arranged reorder grid with a cover badge and a per-photo menu drawn open, and the two registers of error. Nothing sits on a photograph but an opaque control — no scrims, no burnt-in captions (TASTE §9). No file-size cap and no photo count appear anywhere: the first is a product decision nobody has made, the second is the habit that produced "Show all 24 photos". Panel A is the authoritative semantic page; B–G are variants.

A · Light — step 6 of 9 · Photos · arranged, cover set, one photo's menu open
The authoritative page: one h1, real landmarks, a real <form>. Scroll it — the panel is a real scroll container and the action bar is really sticky. The cover badge and the two tile controls are opaque surfaces carrying elevation.onMedia, never translucent discs over a darkened photo. The menu is drawn open because a non-drag reorder path that is only promised is not a path; it carries elevation.popover, the one floating element on this step. Remove from listing is ink — no host card rules on a destructive menu item, and minting a red role here would be inventing one.

What makes a photo work

Four habits, not a standard to pass.

Shoot in daylightOpen the curtains and switch the lights off. A dark room photographs darker than it looks.
Stand in a corner and show the whole roomA wide shot tells a guest how much space there is. A close-up of a cushion does not.
Show what guests will actually findSkip the filters. The photo should match the home on the day they arrive.
Leave people out of the frameGuests are looking at the home. Family, staff and neighbours stay out of shot, including in the background.

G · Narrow — below 820px the grid becomes one column and the action bar wraps, caption first
One column, and the tile chrome does not shrink with it: the badge and the two controls keep their sizes, because a 30px control that is comfortable at 640 is not improved by being 24px at 380. The .nar panel scope mirrors the real @media (max-width:820px) rules exactly.
