GA-021 — states, a11y & cultural-UX coverage

  • States in DOM. Default (fresh, Where active) · typed autocomplete with substring highlight · loading skeleton (rows mirror suggestion geometry, aria-live "Finding more places") · offline (search still works from saved places — names its next step) · first-run empty (no recent searches → "select a city below"). No dead ends.
  • One decision per screen. Where is the only active surface; When and Who are collapsed segments (aria-expanded=false) that open GA-022 and GA-023 — DESIGN §8.4 full-screen accordion, sticky Clear all / Search footer.
  • Cultural-UX (§10). Suggestion rows carry a functional pin / clock glyph only — no ornament, no green-washed fills. The six beta cities (Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore, Peshawar, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi) are stated as plain places, never ranked or sold.
  • A11y (§11). Real <input> with a localized accessible name; placeholder uses text-secondary for AA at 16px; suggestions are <button>s with composed names; ≥44pt targets throughout; focus ring on the active field; full RTL mirror with Urdu accessible names and mirrored chevrons.
  • Copy governance (§7.7). No invented stats, ratings, or superlatives — only place descriptions. Prices in recent searches are placeholders; real figures come from listings.
  • Perf (§12). No imagery in the search surface (text + line icons only) — instant on a struggling connection; skeleton is a flat transform-shimmer, reduced-motion → static.
