Popular questions
How is my payout calculated?

Every booking runs the same chain: gross booking amount, then the service fee (wakala) — a fixed 3% of the gross booking value — then payment processing (MDR), then withholding tax at your ATL filer rate. What's left is your net payout, and every line is shown before every payout.

What happens if my tourism license expires?

We pause your listing automatically until it's renewed — never a surprise, always fixable. It comes back the moment your renewal is in, and nothing else about your account changes.

Do I have to register my guests with the police?

No — SalamStay files this for you. Short-stay guest registration is a routine legal requirement across Punjab, filed through the Hotel Eye system, the same formality any hotel or guest house completes. It's filed automatically within 24 hours of check-in.
