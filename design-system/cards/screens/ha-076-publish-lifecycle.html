Screen — Publish / unpublish / delete listing (HA-076)

The listing's lifecycle controls — reached from the Edit-listing hub (HA-036) in the same editor frame as ha-038/ha-040, and the counterpart to the publish moment ha-033/ha-034 shipped. Three exits, deliberately unequal in weight. Snooze takes a reopen date and is the lightest — it comes back on its own. Unpublish is calm and explicitly reversible: guests can't find or book it, existing bookings are honoured, the calendar, prices and reviews are all kept, and it returns the moment the host republishes (the "comes back the moment…" grammar established by ha-011's auto-pause). Delete is a dignified two-step: a plain consequences list, a stated gate — upcoming bookings must be resolved first, because a listing guests are still booked into cannot be removed — and only then a final confirm, where the destructive token appears exactly once in this card (the ga-080 canon). Case: host Aqib, Cantt View Residence, Rawalpindi Cantonment, PKR 12,000 per night.
