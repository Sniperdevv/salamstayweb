Screen — Block a person (GA-133)

The women's-safety primitive at the heart of Trust & Safety (§9-G) — wired from a profile ⋯ menu and the inbox thread menu (§8.5, §9-E). Blocking is framed as a boundary, not an accusation: it hides, it doesn't accuse (§8.5). Transparency comes first — exactly what blocking does, both ways — and the load-bearing truth is stated plainly and repeated: the blocked person is never notified. The confirm is a calm modal (§8.5), never a scary-red panic step; the action is fully reversible, with an inline unblock and a Settings › Blocked-people manager. Panels: what blocking does (light) · blocked result + undo path (light) · confirm modal + blocked-list manager (dark) · Urdu / RTL.
