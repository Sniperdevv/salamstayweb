Screen — PoA / Wakala Upload + Scope (GA-124)

When the person booking is not the person staying (wakala), the booker adds an authority document and declares their scope in plain language — the scope drives what they're allowed to do on the guest's behalf (ARCH §7.5). Verify-family framing throughout (DESIGN §9-D, §10.6): this is a calm, mutual formality, never an interrogation. The scope choice is a three-option radiogroup — Book only / Book and pay / Book, pay, and cancel if needed — and one line makes clear the person staying still completes their own quick verification too. States: intro, scope declaration, document capture with an always-present Upload from files fallback, review, and the honest track Submitted → In review → Verified — reviewed by a person, not an instant verdict. Needs-attention names a fixable issue, never a judgement. The authority letter is a neutral CSS placeholder — no real document is rendered.
