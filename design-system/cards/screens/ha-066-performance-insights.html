The review summary carries no aggregate score, on purpose. ha-062 ships no host rating and no scoreboard, so inventing a "4.8 average" here would fabricate a number the product does not hold. Instead the surface does the two honest things: it counts themes across a stated window (the host's 20 most recent reviews — his own data, the same class of figure as occupancy) and it quotes the guests verbatim, every quote lifted from a review already shipped on ha-062/ha-077. "Worth a look" is a neutral heading, not a penalty box — the critical mentions sit in the same row shape at the same weight, and the whole section ends by sending the host to the full reviews rather than trusting the summary. Improvement tasks are stated as facts about the host's own listings with a single action each, and none of them argues from demand: "It has 6 photos; your other two homes show 14 and 11" is checkable, "listings with more photos get more bookings" would not be. The 14-day reply window is DESIGN.md §9-J's shipped figure, verbatim from ha-077.

The Eid/Ramadan note is a calendar fact and nothing more. The three periods carry the same plain row treatment at the same weight (DESIGN §10.5/§10.2) on the neutral bg.raised surface — no religious glyph, no lantern, no gold, no brand-green cultural fill, no "festive" skin, and no semantic tint that would read as an alert. The Gregorian dates, the Hijri approximation footnote and the pricing sentence ("Many hosts adjust prices for Eid travel — set yours, or leave it as your base price. Nothing changes automatically.") are all reused verbatim from shipped ha-044. There is deliberately no demand figure — no "bookings rise 40% over Eid", no suggested surge percentage, no "peak season" badge — because SalamStay holds no such data (NEVER #3). The screen states when; the host decides whether.
