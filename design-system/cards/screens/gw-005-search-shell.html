GW-005 — Search: the crawlable shell + the noindex faceted results

Two states of /search (SEO §3.5 · G75 · G76). The shell is noindex, follow and so is every parameter URL — the clean /stays-in-{city} and /stays-in-{city}/{area} pages are the indexable surfaces, and this page's SEO job is to pass crawl to them through six real city links. The registry row calls the shell indexable; GATE 76 (HARD) says it is not — the gate wins and the conflict is parked for the founder in the header comment. Filters are buttons, not links, so browsing cannot mint crawlable facet URLs. Results + map mirror ga-025/ga-026 with the ga-118 relax-a-filter rail.
