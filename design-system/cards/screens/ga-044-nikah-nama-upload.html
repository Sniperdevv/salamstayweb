Screen — Nikah Nama Upload (GA-044)

The couples' verification screen — booking as a couple, both partners complete one short, shared formality. Mutual-formality framing throughout (DESIGN §9-D, §10.6): this is a step every couple takes, never an interrogation to "prove" a marriage. States shown: intro (why + a plain privacy assurance), document capture with an always-present Upload from files fallback, review, and the honest status track Submitted → In review → Verified — reviewed by a person, not an instant-AI verdict. Needs-attention names a fixable photo issue with a retry, never a judgement on the couple. The certificate is a neutral CSS placeholder — no real document is rendered.
