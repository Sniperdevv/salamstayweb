Screen — Data & privacy (GA-080)

Two account-owner rights, handled with dignity (§7.6a: no journey stepper). Export (/account/settings/privacy/export): pick a format, request it, and follow a job-status tracker — requested → preparing → ready to download, with a plain expiry — and an honesty note that your NADRA verification documents are never included; they stay redacted. Delete (/account/settings/delete): a calm consequences list — completed bookings are kept in anonymized form as the law requires — a cooling-off window, and a final confirm that is the only place the destructive token appears. Nothing here is guilt-tripping or panic-red (§10.8). Panels: export request + tracker (light) · delete request + cooling-off confirm (dark) · اردو / RTL export.
