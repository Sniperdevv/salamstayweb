Differences from GA-016, behaviour & S1 gate compliance
  • What makes this GA-017, not the GA-016 first-run panel. GA-016's "first run" is a variant of the signed-in home shown to a brand-new but authenticating user; GA-017 is the standalone route the app mounts when there is no session at all — web entry, listing deep-links, or a signed-out return. Practically: (1) no personalization anywhere — no "Recently viewed", no pre-saved hearts; curated Editor's-picks stand in. (2) The Profile tab and app-bar affordance both read "Log in". (3) Tapping the wishlist heart opens the ask-late sign-in sheet instead of silently saving.
  • Ask-late, never a wall. The entire feed, search, and listing detail are browsable with no auth. Auth is requested only at the point of action (save / book), and the sheet always offers "Keep browsing." No signup interstitial on open (DESIGN §9-H logged-out variant).
  • Cultural = Wifi weight. Women-hosted, Family-friendly, Near masjid render as neutral bg.raised pills at the exact weight of an ordinary amenity — never brand-green filled, never ornamented. Collections are ordinary categories beside "Editor's picks." No-alcohol default stays implicit — never a banner (§10.1–§10.3).
  • Copy governance. Only SEO-RULES §5 registry claims or plain descriptive text; no invented ratings, review counts, or social proof — pre-launch listings carry the "New" chip, never "0.0" or a fake rating (NEVER #3).
  • All content in the DOM. Rails overflow-scroll but every card is present; the sign-in sheet is a real dialog with heading + description + explicit dismiss. No dead ends — the sheet names both next actions.
  • On-palette only. Every color resolves through a var() token; city/listing media are on-palette gradient placeholders (brand + slate + info stops); the one off-role value stays the terracotta saved-heart, unused here since nothing can be saved logged-out.
