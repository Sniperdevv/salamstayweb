Screen — Trusted contacts manager (GA-090)

The general-purpose safety primitive that feeds GA-091's arrival-share Switch — add or remove the people who receive trip-sharing updates, plainly stated as useful for any trip, never branded a "women's" feature on the surface (DESIGN §9-G, §10.8). Initials avatars only, no faces on these fictional contacts, and one row uses the established Urdu-initial avatar in the RTL panel (per the host-profile precedent in GA-027/GA-033). States: the contact list with a what-they-receive transparency panel, the add-contact form, a remove that resolves to an undo toast (no confirm modal), the empty first-run state, and Urdu / RTL.
