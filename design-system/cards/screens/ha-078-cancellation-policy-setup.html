Moderate

Cancel free up to 7 days before check-in and get everything back. Cancel later and part of your stay is refunded, based on how close it is to your arrival.

Example — a 3-night stay at PKR 12,000 a night.
  1. Full refund100%
    Cancel until 7 days before check-in
    PKR 36,000 comes back — everything they paid.
  2. Partial refund50%
    Up to 48 hours before check-in
    Half of the nightly total returns; the service fee (wakala) is kept.
  3. No refund0%
    Inside the last 48 hours, or after check-in
    Nights already reserved for them are non-refundable.
