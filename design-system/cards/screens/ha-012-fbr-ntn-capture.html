Screen — FBR NTN / STRN capture (HA-012)

The host records their FBR National Tax Number (NTN) — and, where it applies, a Sales Tax Registration Number (STRN) — so SalamStay can calculate the correct withholding tax on every payout (host-verify, ARCH §6.9). The NTN is the only required field; the STRN section is optional and explained plainly: for service-tax registered businesses — most individual hosts don't need this. A WHY panel states the one consequence plainly, reusing the shipped withholding concept already verbatim in HA-055. Hosts without an NTN yet have an equal-weight "I don't have an NTN" path that states what that means factually — never a dead end, never a penalty tone. Case: host Aqib, Lahore, Punjab — continuing the lineage shipped in HA-004's Payout step and HA-010. Host setup·Payout

Continuing without an NTN

That's alright — many hosts host without one on file.

What this means
Without an NTN, we can't confirm your Active Taxpayer List status, so withholding is calculated at the non-filer rate until you add one. Non-filers are withheld at roughly double the filer rate, and a withholding certificate is issued for every payout.

FBR's IRIS portal has instructions for registering for an NTN, if you'd like one later.

Add my NTN instead

The NTN field is the only required input; its placeholder pattern mirrors the CNIC-style format most individual hosts already use for their NTN.
