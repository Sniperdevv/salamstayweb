GA-023 — states, cultural-UX & a11y coverage

  • States in DOM. Default (2 adults · 1 child · 0 infants) · minimum-edge (adults at 1 and children at 0 show a disabled − with aria-disabled) · women-only preference on vs off. The Search CTA always carries the live guest summary — no dead end.
  • Just-in-time, no doc-demand (§9-C / ties GA-039). The hint sets expectations ("you'll confirm who's traveling when you book") but asks for nothing here; party-type and any documents are handled later at booking (GA-039), and only what the host needs.
  • Women-only without judgment (§10.7 / §10.8). Presented as an optional, empowering privacy preference — nothing pre-selected, neutral copy, a functional shield-check glyph, never modesty decoration or fear-marketing.
  • A11y (§11). Steppers are ≥44pt buttons with localized names ("Add one adult"); the count is an aria-label'd live value; the preference is a real role="switch" with aria-checked (state carried by position + fill, never color alone); full RTL mirror with the switch thumb travel reversed and Urdu accessible names.
  • Cultural-UX (§10). No ornament; the sheet is calm modern chrome; infant "stays free" is a plain fact, not a sell.
  • Perf (§12). No imagery; a local stepper interaction — instant, transform/opacity-only switch animation, reduced-motion safe.
