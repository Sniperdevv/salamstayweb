Screen — Payment redirect return (GA-116)

The universal catcher for every payment that leaves SalamStay and comes back — wallet app-switch returns (JazzCash / EasyPaisa), Raast bank handoffs, and card 3-D Secure returns (HBL / Stripe). It generalizes GA-055's returned-pending panel into one rail-agnostic reconciliation surface. States over the pay moment: reconciling ("confirming with {rail} — don't close"), still confirming after a wait (honest "this is taking longer than usual" + what happens either way), timeout with the booking held and a retry, and resolved → success. The heart of the screen is the held-booking promise: your dates and price stay held while we reconcile, and money is never silently lost — if the charge went through it books, if it didn't nothing is charged. No wallet-brand colour; emphasis is on-palette only. Dark on the still-confirming wait; Urdu / RTL on reconciling.
