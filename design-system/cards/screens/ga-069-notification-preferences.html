Screen — Notification & channel preferences (GA-069)

The per-type × per-channel matrix at /account/settings/notifications — the guest decides, for each kind of update (booking updates · messages · reminders · offers), which channels carry it: WhatsApp · Push · SMS. WhatsApp is on by default (the established messaging/OTP channel), and Offers ships off — a privacy-respecting default. Below the matrix sits Prayer-time quiet hours, framed as a plain scheduling utility on a neutral surface (§10.3) with a bell/clock glyph — never a religious ornament — and an explicit promise that urgent booking alerts still get through. Panels: matrix with an expanded type row (light) · quiet hours active + a second expanded row (dark) · Urdu / RTL.
