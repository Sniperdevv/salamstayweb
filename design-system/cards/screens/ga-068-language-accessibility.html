Preferences at /account/settings/accessibility, in four distinct sections. Language offers English and اردو — the Urdu row renders in its own Nastaliq script with the §2.2 line-height bump visible, and one quiet line notes more languages are on the way (AR / Pashto are on the roadmap, not built). Currency is a separate section from language: PKR is the default and USD is display-only, under the load-bearing truth line — prices are always booked in PKR (the diaspora / FX context, GA-057). Text size is a slider with a live preview; Appearance is a System / Light / Dark Select and Reduce motion a Switch. No journey stepper — an account screen (§7.6a).
